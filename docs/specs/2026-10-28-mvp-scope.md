# MVP scope

Status: built. The MVP-list item of Phase 0, step 0.5 ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)). It freezes what Phase 1 builds.

## What is frozen

- **The ten top-level factors of the MVP,** from [D21](../decisions.md#d21-mvp-factor-set): Housing and cost, Transport, Daily amenities, Environment, Natural hazards, Safety, Education, Digital connectivity, Community and people, and Health care.
- **Their default weights,** from D14 and the [default weights spec](2026-10-28-default-weights.md).
- **Which measured indicators are in scope:** every one of the 41 that research has taken to `researched` or `spiked` in those ten factors. The other eight top-level factors, and the ideas inside the ten, stay out until Phase 2.

Frozen means that a change needs a decision record in [decisions.md](../decisions.md): adding a top-level factor to the MVP, removing one, or dropping a measured indicator from its scope. Adding a measured indicator to a factor already in the MVP, by the factor recipe, does not.

## What the 41 measured indicators are at launch

Each indicator is one of three things, from what the catalogue says today:

- **Ships (32).** It has a curve, and every dataset it needs is cleared for public builds (D07), or a cleared source can stand in.
- **Needs a curve (5).** Its data is open, and no curve is set yet, because the curve waits for something the build produces. Until then it is shown and not scored ([scoring method](../methodology/scoring.md#7-missing-data-and-data-coverage)).
- **Blocked (4).** A licence or an action only the owner can take stands in the way. It joins the launch if the block lifts in time, and the score does without it until then, with data coverage showing it.
